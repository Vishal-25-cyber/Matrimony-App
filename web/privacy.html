1. Introduction

Pandarathar Matrimony ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of its members. This Privacy Policy outlines what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Profile & Personal Identity: Full Name, gender, date of birth, age, caste, sub-caste, education, occupation, and residential location.
  • Contact Information: 10-digit mobile phone number and optional email address used for secure account verification and profile communication.
  • Horoscope & Astrological Data: Rasi (Moon sign), Nakshatra (Star), Gothram, Chevvai Dosham, Rahu-Ketu Dosham, and planetary placement charts used exclusively for 10 Porutham matrimonial compatibility scoring.
  • Profile Photographs: User-submitted photos for candidate identification, uploaded securely to encrypted Cloudflare R2 object storage.
  • Payment Reference Details: Payer name, payment amount (₹25), and 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) for contact unlock requests. We do not store bank passwords, credit/debit card numbers, or CVVs.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

  • Private by Default: Candidate phone numbers are never displayed publicly. Contact details are only unlocked after an intentional ₹25 verification fee payment.
  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All client-to-server traffic is strictly transmitted over TLS/HTTPS. Database records are encrypted at rest with AES-256 within MongoDB Atlas.
  • No Third-Party Ad Tracking: We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with external advertising networks.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users have the right to request full and permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App: Navigate to Settings (அமைப்புகள்) ➔ Delete Account (கணக்கு நீக்கம்). Upon confirmation, your profile, horoscope data, photos, and saved favorites will be permanently purged from the database.
  • Online Request: You can also submit an external deletion request using our Account Deletion Request Page.
