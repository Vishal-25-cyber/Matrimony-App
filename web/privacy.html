Privacy Policy & Data Protection Statement
Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Pandarathar Matrimony ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Pandarathar Matrimony mobile application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected from registered members seeking matrimonial alliances within the community.

2. Categories of Information Collected

  • Account & Authentication: Name, 10-digit mobile number, optional email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Candidate Profile Details: Gender (Bride / Groom), date of birth, age, marital status, caste, sub-caste, education, occupation, and residential address/district.
  • Horoscope & Astrological Data: Rasi (Moon sign), Nakshatra (Star), Gothram, Chevvai Dosham, Rahu-Ketu Dosham, and planetary astrological placements used exclusively for calculating the traditional 10 Poruthams (திருமணப் பொருத்தங்கள்).
  • Photographs: User-submitted candidate portrait photographs, uploaded to secure Cloudflare R2 object storage.
  • Payment Reference Information: For ₹25 contact unlock requests, we record payer name, payment amount, and 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) for manual or automated verification. We do not collect or store bank account numbers, debit/credit card details, or banking passwords.
  • In-App Notifications & Shortlists: Liked profiles, favorites/shortlists, and unlock notifications generated during app interaction.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Service Providers

  • MongoDB Atlas: Account and matrimonial profile data are stored in a managed cloud database with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Cloudflare R2: User photos and documents are stored on Cloudflare's S3-compatible, geo-redundant storage using encrypted SSL channels.
  • No External Ad Brokers: We do not integrate third-party ad networks (e.g. AdMob) and never sell user data to data brokers.

4. Privacy and Access Controls

  • Private Contact Information: Candidate telephone numbers are never published openly. They remain protected until an interested candidate completes the ₹25 contact unlock request.
  • Verification: Payment records (UTR numbers) are verified before contacts are permanently accessible to the requesting user.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as your profile remains active on the platform. Users have the right to delete their data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings (அமைப்புகள்) ➔ Delete Account (கணக்கு நிரந்தர நீக்கம்). Deletion permanently erases your profile, horoscope data, photos, and saved shortlists from MongoDB Atlas.
  • Web Deletion: You can submit a deletion request via our public Account Deletion Web Page.

6. User Rights

You have the right to inspect, correct, update, or permanently remove your profile data at any time directly through the app interface or by contacting support.
