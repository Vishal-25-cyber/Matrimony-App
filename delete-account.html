How to Request Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, Pandarathar Matrimony provides members two easy ways to delete their account and associated data:

Method 1: Directly inside the Android App (Instant)

  1. Open the Pandarathar Matrimony application.
  2. Tap on the Account (கணக்கு) tab.
  3. Select Settings (அமைப்புகள்).
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account (கணக்கு நிரந்தர நீக்கம்).
  5. Confirm your decision in the warning dialog. Your account and personal records will be immediately and permanently removed from MongoDB Atlas and Cloudflare R2 storage.

Data Retention Policy

  • Data Permanently Deleted: Profile details, mobile number, photo assets, planetary astrological charts, shortlisted candidates, and account credentials.

  • Processing Timeline: In-app deletions take effect immediately. Web submissions are reviewed and processed within 48 hours.
